## background

scope: ISP plant to soc to lab

Four years in the room where an alert lands. Before my security path, I handled the physical security layer: GPON and Active Ethernet, with about 300 endpoints as the sole operator. I was 811 fiber, emergency splicer, and emergency administrator helping keep your internet lights on.

→ ReliaQuest placed me in the SOC queue in Jan 2024 protecting our clients' most critical assets and triaging dark web exposures. Ten months later, I was a full-tier analyst correlating process execution, containing malware, and containing compromised accounts (with the lead-up involving investigating how they occured in the first place). In this role, I learned all major EDR, SIEM, and XDR platforms and memorized modern enterprise configurations and architectures. Knowing the client like the layout of your own home is part of the grind.

→ At WHSmith North America, my role moved from pure responder/investigator to full on operator. I was the sole incident response practitioner responsible for our broader estate. I led Red Team assessments against endpoints and identities, validated our security controls, and patched the holes. In this role, ownership is key, because malware last week, could relate to a different incident next week. I stopped compromised vendor RMM access and help desk impersonations, which are some of my crowning achievements. Imagine stopping a bank robbery while or before it is actually in progress. I also headed our Red Team monthly phishing campaigns, constantly measuring program effectiveness and building progressively engaging phishing templates working alongside the office of the CIO and HR to deliver them.

→ At At-Bay the pipeline was far beyond alerting and pure operating. The game was stopping credential abuse, persistence, and lateral movement. I mitigated BYOVD and precursory ransomware and coordinated with talented DFIR and internal teams to secure their surface area before it became a larger problem. My work also involved tuning groups of alerts that caused recurring false positives through validated process exclusions and XDR rule refinement.

Since Aug 2026 the work is full-time study and lab to harness the experience I've gained in each of these progressively challenging roles to move to bigger projects. HTB Puppet and Mythical are done, along with Intel-Ops Adversary Infrastructure Hunter. CPTS, OSCP, and CRTO are in progress. I've also been to DEF CON 2024 and 2025, and I am ready to apply myself to bigger challenges.

## how_i_work

scope: four rules

Reconstruct, don't assert.

If I say something happened, I can point at the artifact.

Scope before conclusion.

What I tested, what I did not, and what that means for the finding.

Detection is subtraction.

Most tuning work is deciding what does not matter.

The report is the deliverable.

An investigation nobody can read did not happen.
